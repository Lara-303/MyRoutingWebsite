About the studio

A small studio, on purpose.

Anchorline stays deliberately small — six people, senior only, no account managers between you and the work.

"We turned down two acquisitions and a bigger office because both would have meant hiring people to manage people, instead of people to do the work." — Dana Okafor, Founder

How we work

Principle

We say no often.

We take on six studio projects a year. Most weeks, that means turning down good work to protect the projects we've already said yes to.

Principle

Senior people, no bench.

Everyone on your project has run their own studio work before. There's no junior team quietly learning on your budget.

Principle

You own everything we make.

Source files, components, documentation — handed over at the end of every engagement, not licensed back to you.

Principle

Slow is a feature.

We'd rather ship the right brand six weeks later than the wrong one on time. Most clients tell us this after the fact, gratefully.
